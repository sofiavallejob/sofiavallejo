Hola, I'm Sofia.

Audio engineer and researcher from Mexico City, based in Graz, Austria. I turn data into sound and build tools for doing it.

I'm a PhD candidate and University Assistant in Musical Acoustics at the Institute of Electronic Music and Acoustics (IEM), University of Music and Performing Arts Graz. My research is about sonification and the software we use to create sonifications.

Our ICAD 2025 paper on sonification software won the Best Student Paper Award, and I've received the Audio Developer Conference Diversity Scholarship twice.

Before Graz I did an MSc in Sound and Music Computing at the Universitat Pompeu Fabra in Barcelona, Spain, where I modelled analog synth oscillators with neural networks. Before that, I spent a few years in studios and on stages in Mexico.

  3. 2025

    Legacy, active & future software tools in sonification research ★ Best Student Paper

    S. Vallejo Budziszewski, K. Gross-Vogt

    Proc. 30th International Conference on Auditory Display (ICAD 2025), Coimbra, Portugal

    Abstract

    This article provides an overview of the development of active and legacy software tools for sonification. A collection of tools has been compiled and categorized according to research trends suggested in this paper, for the period 1990 to the present. The tools are categorized into five main user scenarios, highlighting key features of sustainable software, and identifying major challenges. The article also outlines a research agenda that considers the application of machine learning for generating intuitive and adaptive sonifications.

  4. 2024

    Modeling the voltage controlled oscillator using augmented neural networks

    S. Vallejo Budziszewski

    MSc thesis, Sound and Music Computing, Music Technology Group, Universitat Pompeu Fabra

    Summary

    A digital model of the VCO of the analog RSF Kobol synthesizer, built with augmented neural networks. The model learns how the oscillator's frequency behaves and how it responds to control voltage, and a phase integrator turns that into a waveform.
